Project overview

This Informatics Skunkworks project, completed with the UW–Madison School of Medicine, explored automated grading of renal cell carcinoma from CT imagery.

Technical work

I optimized a segmentation script to isolate kidney regions and built a pipeline that converted three-dimensional MATLAB volumes into selected two-dimensional slices. We then adapted a pretrained ResNet to the grayscale and textural characteristics of medical imagery.

Outcome

The resulting workflow connected raw volumetric medical data to a standard transfer-learning architecture and established a foundation for non-invasive radiomic classification.
